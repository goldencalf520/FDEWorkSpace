# Treat enterprise identity and audit as core

The initial release will enforce organization, engagement, and role permissions and retain attributable audit events for sensitive viewing, export, confirmation, agent execution, credential use, risk acceptance, and cross-enterprise reuse. Identity uses an OIDC boundary: FDE organization users have local accounts with MFA plus one verified OIDC or Feishu organization adapter, while enterprise participants combine expiring invitations with email-code or Feishu verification; SAML, additional enterprise identity providers, and SMS remain adapters. A tightly controlled emergency administrator is audited, and identity, authorization, and audit semantics cannot be deferred because they determine whether evidence and automation can be trusted.
