# Govern custom capabilities by trust, dependencies, and version

Organizations may add custom Skills, MCP servers, agents, models, adapters, and tools, but every capability carries a trust level that constrains sensitive data, credentials, and external effects. Its prerequisites must be evaluated before execution, and important outcomes retain exact capability-version locks so upgrades are impact-reviewed, reversible, and unable to rewrite historical results. An incomplete declaration remains an experimental capability; promotion to organization-verified requires permission review, dependency checks, an isolated trial, and accountable approval, while a failed health check may suspend use but cannot silently restore elevated permissions. Verified custom capabilities may be published to an organization-private catalog with publisher, compatibility, and withdrawal metadata, but do not enter a cross-organization public marketplace by default.
