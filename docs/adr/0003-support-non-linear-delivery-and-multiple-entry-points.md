# Support non-linear delivery and multiple entry points

The seven parts of the product are delivery areas rather than a single mandatory sequence. An engagement declares one of three entry modes: discovery from meetings and interviews, requirements from an organized brief, or takeover of an existing product and codebase. Imported material remains a supplied baseline rather than silently becoming confirmed or verified; in takeover mode the workbench must first derive a current-state model for FDE correction before comparing the existing product with the target prototype. Work may return to any upstream area, and an upstream change must mark dependent downstream outcomes for re-examination. This accommodates mature engagements and inherited implementations while preserving a common delivery model.
