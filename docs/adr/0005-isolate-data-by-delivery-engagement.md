# Isolate data by delivery engagement

Each delivery engagement is the minimum boundary for permissions, evidence, domain-knowledge matching, decisions, artifacts, code, tests, and reviews. Recognition context and correction memory are engagement-scoped by default; policy may promote confirmed terminology to the same enterprise or to an anonymous industry context. The FDE organization controls reuse through policy rather than item-by-item approval: reuse of source material across engagements of the same enterprise is policy-controlled, while cross-enterprise reuse defaults to abstractions such as industry patterns, solution profiles, and aggregated performance results. Raw recordings, documents, enterprise identity, business data, and source code must not be injected automatically across enterprise boundaries; a contractually permitted exception requires a specifically scoped, audited cross-enterprise override.
