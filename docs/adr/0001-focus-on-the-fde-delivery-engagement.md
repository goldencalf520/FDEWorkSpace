# Focus on the FDE delivery engagement

The workbench will serve the solution provider's FDE and delivery lead as its primary day-to-day users. It is an evidence-to-delivery operating system organized around a delivery engagement, while enterprise participants join through focused evidence, confirmation, and review interactions rather than operating another full project-management suite. This focus is preferred over building separate meeting, prototyping, and coding products because the product's differentiator is traceability across the complete delivery chain.
