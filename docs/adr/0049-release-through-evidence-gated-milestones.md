# Release through evidence-gated milestones

Delivery will proceed through an architecture spike, an internal alpha spanning the three entry modes and a constrained seven-area loop, three differentiated real-project pilots, and an externally operated beta. The first alpha slice uses the existing diagnosis-demo scenario to carry a sixty-minute interview through findings, one current and target process, prototype, a thin implemented capability, evidence-backed testing, review, and a Demo Snapshot. Each milestone produces a runnable vertical product rather than disconnected horizontal layers. External beta is blocked until all pilots complete, at least two responsible FDEs choose continued use, Development Baseline Lead Time improves by at least thirty percent in the measured pilot context, the agreed recognition, performance, testing, isolation, security, recovery, and zero-leakage criteria pass, and no blocking failure remains.
