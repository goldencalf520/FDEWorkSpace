# Act as the delivery control plane

The workbench will own delivery meaning, decision relationships, state, permissions, and traceability while integrating with rather than replacing meeting platforms, recording devices, transcription models, source-control hosts, coding agents, IDEs, browser automation, test frameworks, CI/CD services, project-management systems, and model runtimes. It will provide adapters and coordinate their inputs and outputs, but it will not become a foundation-model vendor, Git host, or general browser engine. This boundary concentrates the product on cross-tool delivery orchestration and avoids rebuilding the specialist tools used to produce individual artifacts.
