# Gate demo readiness with evidence

Customer demonstrations will use versioned readiness templates specialized from the actual demo scenario, environment, integrations, capabilities, and participants. A demo cannot be declared ready while a blocking item remains; a named FDE may accept a documented non-blocking risk, but the decision must preserve item ownership, completion evidence, and at least one attributable rehearsal result rather than relying on a checklist percentage or presenter assurance. Machine-verifiable items will be checked automatically while human-only preparations require accountable confirmation, and a material change to the product version, data, accounts, integration configuration, or script invalidates the affected evidence. Every rehearsal or customer demonstration creates its own demo run without enabling recording by default, and discovered problems become linked remediation items that must be re-verified before readiness is restored.
